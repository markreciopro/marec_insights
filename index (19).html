How an AI scheduling or forecasting tool is validated
  • Backtest against your own actuals, not vendor demo data
  • Work-rule check for union agreements, penalty pay, and overtime
  • Edge cases such as conventions, holidays, and demand shifts
  • Fairness review of how hours and shifts are spread
  • Drift monitoring after go-live
Checklist: questions to ask your scheduling-AI vendor
  1. Was it tested on data like mine? Ask whether accuracy was measured on properties similar to yours or only on vendor demo data.
  2. Can it apply our work rules? Union agreements, penalty pay, and overtime thresholds should be configurable, and you should be able to see where each rule is set.
  3. How does it handle surprises? Conventions, holidays, weather, and sudden demand changes.
  4. Can hours and shifts be audited for fairness? You should be able to see how hours are spread across staff.
  5. Who approves a schedule before it is published? Look for a human approval step and a log of every change.
  6. How is accuracy monitored after go-live? Ask what triggers a retrain or a rollback.
  7. Where does your data go? What the vendor stores, what it uses for training, and where it is hosted.
  8. What notice or bargaining steps does your contract require? Ask what documentation the vendor can provide to support them. This checklist is general guidance, not legal advice.

Data security

How MAREC protects your property data

MAREC cleans your data locally, models on safe synthetic copies, and runs the final code on your real data behind your firewall.

See the 3-step process
  1. Clean locally. PMS, payroll, and revenue exports are cleaned and stripped of personal identifiers on local hardware.
  2. Model on a synthetic twin. A safe copy (SAFE_*.csv) with differential-privacy noise keeps revenue shapes and labor curves, so cloud AI and open-source tools can build models without seeing real figures.
  3. Run on real data locally. The generated SQL, Python, or R scripts run in VS Code or Jupyter behind your firewall. Live data never leaves your property.

Common questions

Hospitality labor analytics FAQ

What does MAREC Insights do?

MAREC Insights is a Las Vegas hospitality analytics practice focused on labor. It forecasts demand and staffing, controls overtime and labor cost, validates AI scheduling and forecasting tools, and connects labor to revenue on executive dashboards.

Who is MAREC Insights for?

The best fits are independent, boutique, and island resorts; event, banquet, and convention operators; staffing and consulting firms; and management companies or small portfolios. Large casino resorts engage MAREC for specific projects, such as validating a scheduling tool or analyzing event labor. Remote engagements are available anywhere.

How can analytics reduce labor costs at a resort or hotel?

Analytics matches staffing to forecast demand by department and shift, shows where overtime and idle hours build up, and checks schedules against union and work rules before they are published. Because labor is viewed next to revenue, cost cuts do not quietly hurt service or sales. In earlier operations work, analysis of scheduling patterns and labor utilization helped reduce operating expenses by 15% for service and event crews.

What is the 30-day pilot, and how is it priced?

The pilot has three steps: a 20-minute alignment call to scope margin goals, a data and AI readiness audit, and a 30-day action model and rollout blueprint built on your own data. Pricing is a fixed fee or tied to measured savings, set on the scoping call. Work can also be a project, contract, fractional support, or white-label deliverables.

Can you validate an AI scheduling or forecasting tool?

Yes. A validation covers a backtest against your own historical actuals, a work-rule check for union agreements and overtime thresholds, edge cases such as conventions and holidays, a fairness review of how hours are spread across staff, and drift monitoring after go-live.

What are design-partner pilots?

A small number of pilots are offered at a reduced fee to operators willing to share baseline numbers and, if they are satisfied with the results, a short case study and approved quote. You review and approve anything published, an anonymized version is available, and there is no publicity obligation if the results fall short.

What data do I need to start?

Most projects start with exports you already have: PMS or revenue reports, payroll and timekeeping, schedules, and event or banquet booking data. A data readiness audit confirms what is usable before any modeling begins.

How is my property data protected?

Sensitive exports such as PMS, payroll, and revenue files are cleaned and stripped of personal identifiers locally. Safe synthetic versions are used for cloud and AI modeling, and the resulting code runs on your real data behind your own firewall. Work is delivered under NDA and within IT-compliant architecture.

Who will work on my project?

MAREC Insights is a specialist practice founded by Mark Anthony Recio, who does the analysis and consulting work directly. Staffing talent for rollouts is available through partner Synergy Staffing Solutions, and white-label arrangements are available for staffing and consulting firms.

Does MAREC do revenue management?

MAREC analyzes revenue measures such as RevPAR, GOPPAR, and booking pace so staffing follows demand and labor is judged next to revenue. It is not positioned as an outsourced revenue management or pricing service.
