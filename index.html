ABOUT THE PRACTICE

About MAREC Insights

Founded by Mark Anthony Recio, MBA, MAREC Insights is a specialized independent consulting practice operating at the intersection of HR analytics, business analytics, data science, and hospitality operations. Serving small and midsized businesses, boutique hotels, and independent resorts, the practice transforms complex operational and revenue metrics into actionable financial leverage.

With deep expertise in statistical modeling, predictive labor yield analysis, and regulatory compliance (FMLA/ADA), MAREC Insights bridges the gap between raw organizational data and executive-level decision making.

Data Science & Business Analytics

01

VOLATILITY

Unpredictable Seasonal Demand Swings

SECURE IMPLEMENTATION & DATA SECURITY

How We Build a Trusted Partner Framework

To secure critical operational and revenue data without friction, engagements are structured like professional IT implementations built on privacy-first foundations.

01

PROTECTION FIRST

Sign an NDA First

Always have a standard NDA ready before diving into particulars. It signals that client privacy is handled with large-firm rigor.

BENEFIT

Immediate alignment on strict confidentiality.

02

ZERO RISK

Request "Read-Only" Access

Never request write access or booking modification rights. Access is restricted strictly to read-only PMS or POS exports.

BENEFIT

Removes fear of accidental data modification.

03

AUTOMATED BRIDGE

The "Data Export" Bridge

For risk-averse teams, use automated daily or weekly CSV/Excel raw file exports instead of direct software integration.

BENEFIT

Lowest-friction way to initiate analytics.

04

PIPELINE AUTOMATION

Python, R Programming & Power BI Integration

Leverage technical pipelines to ingest exports and populate Power BI dashboards seamlessly without touching core software.

BENEFIT

Zero disruption to daily property operations.

TECH: Python · R Programming · SQL · Power BI
