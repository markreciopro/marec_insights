How an engagement works

One integrated approach. HR analytics measures what is happening, people analytics explains why and what comes next, and management consulting turns both into a decision and a plan. Each of the five phases uses all three together.

  1. Frame the decision

    • HR Analytics

      Define each metric once, with a data dictionary everyone signs off on.

    • People Analytics

      Write the questions as testable hypotheses, such as where turnover is concentrated and why.

    • Consulting

      Agree the decision, the decision-maker, success measures and timeline.

  2. Baseline and environment

    • HR Analytics

      Audit HRIS, payroll, hiring and absence data, then set the baseline for turnover, hiring and cost.

    • People Analytics

      Segment the workforce by role, tenure, manager and location to see where patterns differ.

    • Consulting

      Run PESTEL, SWOT, environmental analysis and Porter's Five Forces on the outside and inside picture.

  3. Diagnose

    • HR Analytics

      Benchmark and trend the core metrics to confirm what is actually happening.

    • People Analytics

      Use hypothesis testing and driver analysis to separate real causes from noise.

    • Consulting

      Apply VRIN, the BCG Matrix and Blue Ocean Strategy to judge advantages, investments and new options.

  4. Decide

    • HR Analytics

      Put a cost and benefit on each option using validated numbers.

    • People Analytics

      Model scenarios and risk, such as attrition forecasts, with fairness and bias checks.

    • Consulting

      Rank options with RICE, state the trade-offs, and write the strategic plan with owners and dates.

  5. Act and monitor

    • HR Analytics

      Deliver a scorecard and dashboard in Power BI or Tableau, on a fixed reporting rhythm.

    • People Analytics

      Monitor model accuracy and drift, and keep a person approving any AI-assisted output.

    • Consulting

      Support change management, hold review meetings, and adjust the plan as results come in.

The 30-day diagnostic covers phases one to four and leaves you with a ranked plan. Phase five is optional ongoing support.

Generative AI

Use generative AI safely

AI speeds up analysis and drafting. In HR and strategy work it also touches personal data and decisions about people, so it needs rules before it needs scale.

Safeguards built into every engagement

  • Protect data. Remove personal identifiers locally, and never paste confidential records into public tools
  • Keep a person in charge. A named reviewer approves anything that affects hiring, pay, or scheduling
  • Verify outputs. Check figures, sources and logic, since AI can sound sure and be wrong
  • Test for bias. Compare outcomes across groups before and after launch
  • Keep an audit trail. Record prompts, versions, approvals and changes
  • Monitor for drift. Re-check accuracy on a set schedule, with a rollback plan

What you receive

  • AI use policy
  • Approved and restricted use cases
  • Data-handling rules
  • Vendor question checklist
  • Validation report
  • Team training session

General guidance, not legal advice. Employment, privacy and labor rules vary, so your counsel should review policies.

Checklist: questions to ask any AI vendor
  1. Was it tested on data like mine? Ask whether accuracy was measured on organizations like yours, not only on demo data.
  2. Can it follow our rules? Policies, labor agreements and thresholds should be configurable and visible.
  3. Can outcomes be audited for fairness? You should be able to see how results are spread across people and groups.
  4. Who approves before an output is used? Look for a human approval step and a log of every change.
  5. How is accuracy monitored after go-live? Ask what triggers a retrain or a rollback.
  6. Where does our data go? What the vendor stores, what it uses for training, and where it is hosted.

Data security

How MAREC protects your property data

MAREC cleans your data locally, models on safe synthetic copies, and runs the final code on your real data behind your firewall.

See the 3-step process
  1. Clean locally. PMS, payroll, and revenue exports are cleaned and stripped of personal identifiers on local hardware.
  2. Model on a synthetic twin. A safe copy (SAFE_*.csv) with differential-privacy noise keeps revenue shapes and labor curves, so cloud AI and open-source tools can build models without seeing real figures.
  3. Run on real data locally. The generated SQL, Python, or R scripts run in VS Code or Jupyter behind your firewall. Live data never leaves your property.

Common questions

Independent AI validation FAQ

What does MAREC Insights do?

MAREC Insights provides independent people analytics and AI validation. It checks that scheduling, forecasting and HR tools are accurate, fair and consistent with your rules, then turns the findings into decisions using HR analytics, people analytics, data analysis and management consulting frameworks.

How is MAREC different from scheduling or workforce software vendors?

Software platforms automate scheduling, forecasting and reporting inside their own product. MAREC does not sell software, so it has no reason to favor a tool. It tests a tool's output on your own historical data and rules, and it works alongside the platform you already use.

Do you replace my scheduling, HRIS or BI tools?

No. MAREC works alongside them. The tools stay in place while their output is tested, documented and monitored.

What does an independent AI validation include?

A backtest against your own historical actuals, a rule and policy check for labor agreements and overtime, edge cases such as peak seasons and holidays, a fairness review of how outcomes are spread across people and groups, and a monitoring plan. The result is a go, fix or hold recommendation with the reasons.

Who is MAREC Insights for?

Multi-property operators and management companies, event and banquet operators, staffing and consulting firms, unionized Las Vegas properties, and employers adopting AI in HR. Independent, boutique and island resorts can engage remotely for specific projects.

What is the difference between HR analytics and people analytics?

HR analytics measures core HR functions such as headcount, turnover, hiring, pay and absence. People analytics goes further and tests what drives outcomes like retention, performance and engagement, so leaders can act on causes rather than only report on numbers.

Which management consulting tools do you use?

RICE for prioritizing initiatives, SWOT, PESTEL and environmental analysis for the external and internal picture, Porter's Five Forces for industry structure, VRIN for competitive advantage, Blue Ocean Strategy for new market space, the BCG Matrix for portfolio choices, hypothesis testing for evidence, and strategic planning to tie it together. The tool is chosen for the question, not the other way around.

How do you use generative AI safely?

Sensitive files are cleaned of personal identifiers locally, AI is used on synthetic or aggregated data, outputs are checked by a person before anyone relies on them, and bias, privacy and accuracy are reviewed before and after go-live. Nothing confidential is pasted into public AI tools.

What is the 30-day diagnostic, and how is it priced?

It has three steps: a 20-minute alignment call, a data and AI readiness audit, and a 30-day analysis and action plan built on your own data. Pricing is a fixed fee or tied to measured results, set on the scoping call. Work can also be a project, contract, fractional support or white-label deliverables.

What data do I need to start, and how is it protected?

Most projects start with exports you already have: HRIS, payroll and timekeeping, schedules, and booking or event data. Sensitive exports are cleaned of personal identifiers locally, safe synthetic versions are used for cloud and AI modeling, and the resulting code runs on your real data behind your own firewall, under NDA.

Do you still work with hospitality?

Yes. Hospitality and shift-based workforces are the proof vertical, built on nearly five years of running scheduling, payroll and event staffing in Las Vegas. That experience shapes how tools are tested against real work rules, peaks and union agreements.
