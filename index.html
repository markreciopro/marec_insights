One process, from question to decision

Every engagement follows the same six phases. They combine the people analytics cycle, the four-step analytics ladder (descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, prescriptive) and three lenses: HR analytics measures what is happening, people analytics explains why, and consulting turns both into a decision. The 30-day diagnostic covers phases one to five, and the quarterly review covers phase six.

Use generative AI safely

In HR and workforce decisions, AI touches personal data and decisions about people, so it needs rules before it needs scale.

Safeguards in every engagement

  • Protect data. Remove personal identifiers locally
  • Keep a person in charge. A named reviewer approves outputs that affect people
  • Verify outputs. Check figures, sources and logic
  • Test for bias. Compare outcomes across groups
  • Keep an audit trail. Record versions, approvals and changes
  • Monitor for drift. Re-check accuracy, with a rollback plan

Questions to ask any AI vendor

  1. Was it tested on data like mine?
  2. Can it follow our rules?
  3. Can outcomes be audited for fairness?
  4. Who approves before an output is used?
  5. How is accuracy monitored after go-live?
  6. Where does our data go?

Independent AI validation FAQ

What does MAREC Insights do, and who is it for?

MAREC Insights provides independent people analytics and AI validation. It checks that scheduling, forecasting and HR tools are accurate, fair and consistent with your rules, then turns the findings into decisions using HR analytics, people analytics, data analysis and management consulting. It suits multi-property operators, event and banquet operators, staffing and consulting firms, unionized Las Vegas properties, and employers adopting AI in HR.

How is MAREC different from software vendors, and do you replace my tools?

Software platforms automate scheduling, forecasting and reporting inside their own product. MAREC does not sell software, so it has no reason to favor a tool. It works alongside the platforms you already use and tests their output on your own data and rules.

What does an independent AI validation include?

A backtest against your own historical actuals, a rule and policy check for labor agreements and overtime, edge cases such as peak seasons and holidays, a fairness review of how outcomes are spread across people and groups, and a monitoring plan. The result is a go, fix or hold recommendation with the reasons.

What is the difference between HR analytics and people analytics?

HR analytics measures core HR functions such as headcount, turnover, hiring, pay and absence. People analytics goes further and tests what drives outcomes like retention, performance and engagement, so leaders can act on causes rather than only report on numbers.

Which management consulting frameworks do you use?

RICE for prioritizing initiatives, SWOT, PESTEL and environmental analysis for the external and internal picture, Porter's Five Forces for industry structure, VRIN for competitive advantage, Blue Ocean Strategy for new market space, the BCG Matrix for portfolio choices, hypothesis testing for evidence, and strategic planning to tie it together. The framework is chosen for the question, not the other way around.

How is my data protected, and how do you use generative AI safely?

Sensitive exports are cleaned of personal identifiers locally. Safe synthetic versions are used for cloud and AI modeling, and the resulting code runs on your real data behind your own firewall, under NDA. Outputs are checked by a person before anyone relies on them, and nothing confidential is pasted into public AI tools.

What is the 30-day diagnostic, and how is it priced?

It has three steps: a 20-minute alignment call, a data and AI readiness audit, and a 30-day analysis and action plan built on your own data. Most projects start with exports you already have, such as HRIS, payroll, timekeeping and schedules. Pricing is a fixed fee or tied to measured results, set on the scoping call.
